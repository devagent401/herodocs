## Purpose
The call log for the whole team: direction, outcome, duration, linked lead and recording status. Agents and team leads use it at `/calls`; each call opens at `/calls/:callId`, and `/calls/search` is the transcript search. Saved views: All calls, Today, Inbound, Outbound, Missed, With transcript, My calls and a board by outcome.

## What works with the real API
- The call list loads server call logs through the table-query source (`calls`) in live mode.
- Call detail shows the transcript and AI call summary from the AI and assistant services once the recording is stored.
- Call detail loads the recording through a short-lived playback link from the AI service (`/recordings/playback`).
- Outbound calls, hang-up, answer, decline, transfer (including warm transfer) and mute/hold are sent to the telephony service in live mode.
- Calls only really connect when the Asterisk service, the Twilio Elastic SIP trunk and the browser softphone are running (see ADR-0005). Otherwise nothing rings.

## Not finished yet
- Disposition and notes saving: the live write path was not verified.
- Transcript search (`/calls/search`) is the in-browser mock engine over seeded transcripts and is labelled WHISPER MOCK. There is no server-side search yet.
- `/calls/simulate` (inbound simulator, button on the list) is demo only.
- Outside live mode the call detail shows MOCK badges (transcription request, local recording storage).
- Real-call quality in production is not verified here.
