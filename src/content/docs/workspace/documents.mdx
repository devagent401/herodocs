## Purpose
One list of contracts, offers and uploaded files, with their status and signature state. Sales staff use it at `/documents` to preview and upload. Views: All, Awaiting signature, Signed, Contracts, Uploaded, Offers and Void (replaced).

## What works with the real API
- The list loads through the table-query source (`documents`) in live mode.
- Upload sends the file to the documents service (`/documents/upload`); the preview downloads it again.
- Generated documents open in a live preview modal.
- Signature requests go through the documents service (DocuSeal client).

## Not finished yet
- Without live mode the page shows demo documents and a MOCK badge on Upload.
- Real e-signature depends on a configured DocuSeal setup; not verified.
- Template editing is under Settings, not on this page.
