## Purpose
A library of recorded calls where quality auditors and team leads listen, read transcripts and ask questions about calls. Route: `/recordings`, detail at `/recordings/:id`.

## What works with the real API
- The list loads recordings from the AI service (`/recordings`).
- The detail page loads the transcript (`/recordings/<call>/transcript`) and a short-lived audio link for playback.
- The ask box posts the question to the AI service (`/ask`) and shows the answer with citations.
- The AI call summary is read from the assistant service outputs (`/ai/outputs`); the card is still labelled MOCK in the UI.
- Recording itself is done server-side by Asterisk and needs the telephony stack running.

## Not finished yet
- Without the live services the page shows seeded demo recordings (labelled demo/MOCK).
- Transcription quality and the legal review of recording (consent, GDPR) are open items in ADR-0005.
- Ask answers depend on AI credits; the fallback without an LLM is limited.
