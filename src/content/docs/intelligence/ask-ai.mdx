## Purpose
Ask AI opens a side panel (shortcut Ctrl J) from the sidebar or the top bar. Any member with the AI permission can ask about a lead or the workspace. There is no full page: the sidebar entry opens the panel (route `/ask-ai` only marks the entry).

## What works with the real API
- In live mode the chat streams from the AI service through the API proxy (`/v1/assistant/ai/chat`).
- The server checks permission and tenant, reserves and charges real credits, and audits the call.
- The panel shows the credits used and the new balance, and shows proposed actions as cards.
- Out-of-credits errors lead to the buy prompt.

The same AI service powers the AI tab on a contact record (summaries and actions, same credits).
