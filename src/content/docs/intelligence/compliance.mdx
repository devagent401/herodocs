## Purpose
Compliance shows violations found by the guard (for example asking for a TAN or a foreign IBAN) and lets admins dismiss, escalate or confirm them and unlock locked agents. Route: `/compliance`. Tabs: Violations, Rules, Agents (risk score and unlock), Settings, Audit.

## What works with the real API
- In live mode the queue, locks, stats and audit load from the guard service (`/v1/guard/violations`, `/locks`, `/stats`, `/audit`).
- Dismiss, escalate and confirm, and Unlock agent are server calls.
- Policy settings load and save through `/v1/guard/policy`, and the sentence tester uses `/v1/guard/policy/test` (may spend AI credits).
- The guard service exists in the repo with its own HTTP routes.

## Not finished yet
- The live lock screen and in-call blocking depend on the telephony and mail paths calling the guard; not verified end to end.
- Demo scenarios (blocked email, locked agent) run only on the in-browser engine.
- Notification of admins on high violations was not verified.
