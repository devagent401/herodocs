## Purpose
Admins manage workspace members, teams and role permissions. Route: `/settings/members`; roles are at `/settings/roles`, teams at `/settings/teams` and projects & brands at `/settings/projects`. The role page has a permission matrix and an "act as" preview.

## What works with the real API
- Members, roles, teams and projects load from `/v1/settings/*`.
- Creating or editing roles, teams and members, and deleting roles or teams, are sent to the same API.
- Inviting a member uses `POST /v1/invites` and respects the plan's seat limit.

## Not finished yet
- In demo mode invitations are simulated (MOCK, no email sent).
- The permission matrix and the "act as" role preview are driven by the local store; whether every toggle is enforced server-side is not verified.
- Changes sync through a queue, so the page is optimistic rather than request-by-request.
