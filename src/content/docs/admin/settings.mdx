## Purpose
`/settings` opens a left list of sections. The sidebar items AI & credits, Appearance, Team & roles and Telephony are shortcuts into it. This page lists the rest. Route: `/settings/:section`. Most sections are hidden from roles without the matching permission.

## What works with the real API
- **Tenant, Projects & brands, Members, Teams, Roles & permissions**: loaded and saved through the settings API. Members are invited through the invite API.
- **Branding & team**: reads and saves tenant branding.
- **Phone numbers**: see the Telephony page.
- **Inbound routing & queues, Absences & substitutes, Recording & transcripts**: live panels in live mode (queues and absences use the telephony and absences routes), MOCK pages in demo mode.
- **AI attributes**: AI-filled fields, saved through the assistant service.
- **Service connections, Campaigns (Instantly)**: show live service status.

## Not finished yet
- **Product blueprints, Privacy & retention**: live panel in live mode, MOCK page in demo mode. Not verified end to end.
- **Templates, Lead routing, Load rule, Notifications, Booking, Telegram, Collaboration, Compliance guard, Warm-up defaults, Desktop devices, Data model**: each belongs to a feature page and is only partly checked. Status is not verified here.
- **Integrations & providers**: see the Integrations page.
