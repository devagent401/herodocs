## Purpose
Only platform owners (the people who run HeroCloud itself) see this page. It manages every customer workspace (tenant). Route: `/platform` (tabs: Tenants, Plans, Announcements, Platform audit), with a detail page per tenant at `/platform/tenants/:id` (tabs: Overview, Members, Integrations, Branding, Audit). New owners also meet `/onboarding` and invite links at `/invite/:token`. It does not appear in the sidebar for anyone else.

## What works with the real API
- Loads the overview and the tenant list from the platform API.
- Creates a tenant and invites its owner.
- Edits tenant details, branding and integration assignments; suspends, reactivates, exports and deletes a tenant after confirmation.
- Starts and ends impersonation (with a reason).
- Loads and edits plans, and creates or switches off announcements.
- Loads the platform audit and verifies it; grants monthly credits.
