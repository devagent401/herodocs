## Purpose
Every person can change how the app looks. You pick the theme and where the main menu lives, and you can reset your personal table layouts. Route: `/settings/appearance`. No special permission is needed.

## What works with the real API
- Nothing. This page has no backend call by design.

## Not finished yet
- Choices are stored only in this browser (local preferences). They do not follow you to another device or browser.
- The customer portal always stays light.
