## Purpose
AI features (summaries, AI attributes, Ask AI) are paid in credits. This page shows the balance, usage and ledger, and lets admins set limits. Route: `/settings/ai-credits`. Everyone with the AI permission can view it. Buying needs the credit-purchase permission.

## What works with the real API
- Loads the balance, ledger and usage by day, user and feature from the assistant service.
- Loads the price list from the assistant service.
- Saves the AI controls (kill switch, per-feature toggles, monthly cap) through the assistant service.
- Starts a credit purchase after you confirm the price, and records the order on the server.

- Platform owners also get an AI platform billing page at `/ai/platform` (workspaces, credit grants, price list). In live mode it loads from the assistant service.

## Not finished yet
- Payment is a MOCK checkout: the "Pay (MOCK)" button books the credits without real money. The real Stripe checkout needs the Stripe key to be configured. Not verified against a live Stripe account.
- In demo mode the whole page runs on the in-browser store.
- The credit price in euros is shown as a fixed retail rate, labelled MOCK billing.
